Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Legal Entity Management, Corporate Governance, Entity Compliance & Organizational Structures
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS/hosted platforms and open-source projects for Entity Management. These tools help organizations maintain legal-entity records, corporate structures, ownership information, directors and officers, statutory filings, compliance deadlines, governance documents, organizational charts, and entity lifecycle information.

Examples include Athennian, Diligent Entities, CSC Entity Management, Corporatek, Viewpoint, Harbor Compliance, GEMS by Computershare, EntityKeeper, CT Corporation hCue, and Athennian Governance.

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with open-source entity-management applications, corporate compliance trackers, governance systems, entity-data frameworks, organizational-structure tools, GRC platforms, and reusable open-source building blocks for creating self-hosted entity-management systems.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

SaaS/Hosted Platforms

Athennian
Cloud-based entity management and Governance Ops platform for legal, tax, finance, and compliance teams managing complex entity structures, ownership, governance documents, appointments, and compliance.

Diligent Entities
Entity management and governance platform for maintaining entity records, corporate structures, compliance obligations, governance documents, and related workflows.

CSC Entity Management
Enterprise entity management platform combining centralized entity records, ownership, governance documents, compliance calendars, organizational charts, reporting, and corporate-service workflows.

Corporatek / EnGlobe
Enterprise legal entity management platform for entity tracking, subsidiary governance, director and officer management, regulatory filings, ownership structures, organizational charts, and compliance across jurisdictions.

Viewpoint
Corporate entity and governance management software for maintaining legal-entity information, corporate records, compliance activities, and organizational structures.

Harbor Compliance
Compliance and entity-management platform combining business registrations, registered-agent services, annual reports, licenses, and corporate compliance management.

GEMS by Computershare
Global entity management system that centralizes entity data, compliance workflows, governance information, reporting, documents, and organizational structures.

EntityKeeper
Entity management and compliance platform providing a centralized source of truth for entities, documents, ownership structures, organizational charts, deadlines, reporting, and governance records.

CT Corporation hCue
Corporate compliance and entity-management technology from CT Corporation for managing legal entities, corporate records, compliance obligations, and related filing activities.

Athennian Governance
Governance-focused capabilities from Athennian for managing entity records, people and appointments, controls, governance documents, ownership structures, tasks, and compliance.

Corporify
Legal entity management platform for managing corporate structures, entity data, governance documentation, compliance, ownership, and organizational relationships.

DiliTrust
Legal management and governance platform with capabilities for corporate governance, entity management, legal documents, compliance workflows, and corporate records.

SimpleLegal
Legal operations platform that can centralize corporate legal information and connect entity-related workflows with broader legal operations.

Athennian Entity Management
Unified entity and people records, ownership structures, governance controls, documents, appointments, tasks, reports, and AI-assisted entity workflows.

Legal Entity Identifier Services
Global LEI infrastructure used by organizations and entity-management systems to identify legal entities through standardized Legal Entity Identifiers.

Open-Source GitHub Projects

Entity Compliance Tracker
Open-source corporate entity registry for in-house legal teams with entity and subsidiary records, compliance calendars, organizational charts, and filing-deadline reminders.

EIOS — Entity Information Operating System
Open framework for entity-centered information management covering entity information, events, governance, access, knowledge, retrieval, and portable self-hosted entity information systems.

Probo
Open-source, self-hostable GRC platform for managing governance, risk, compliance, controls, vendors, audits, privacy, evidence, and organizational compliance entities.

Corvus
Open-source entity lifecycle management and bulk-operation project using entity-oriented data models and standards such as JSON-LD and NGSI-LD.

ERPNext
Open-source ERP platform that can be extended with custom legal-entity, subsidiary, ownership, governance, compliance, document, workflow, and reporting modules.

Frappe Framework
Open-source application framework suitable for building custom entity-management applications with structured records, permissions, workflows, reports, documents, and audit trails.

Odoo Community
Open-source business application platform that can be extended to manage companies, subsidiaries, contacts, documents, approvals, compliance workflows, and corporate records.

OpenCorporates
Open-source projects and public corporate-data infrastructure associated with OpenCorporates, providing tools and data around companies and corporate registries.

OpenRefine
Open-source data-cleaning and transformation tool useful for consolidating fragmented entity records, corporate registries, ownership data, and compliance datasets.

CKAN
Open-source data management platform that can be used to publish and manage structured corporate-registry and entity datasets.

Apache Superset
Open-source analytics platform suitable for building dashboards around entity portfolios, jurisdiction coverage, compliance deadlines, ownership structures, and governance metrics.

Metabase
Open-source business intelligence platform for reporting and analytics across corporate entity, ownership, compliance, and governance datasets.

Grafana
Open-source visualization platform useful for entity-management dashboards, compliance calendars, governance KPIs, filing status, and operational metrics.

PostgreSQL
Open-source relational database suitable as the core system of record for custom legal-entity databases, ownership structures, governance records, compliance deadlines, and document metadata.

Neo4j
Graph database technology useful for representing complex relationships between parent companies, subsidiaries, shareholders, directors, officers, beneficial owners, and affiliated entities.

Apache AGE
Open-source graph extension for PostgreSQL that can provide graph-based corporate ownership and relationship modeling while retaining PostgreSQL as the underlying database.

ArangoDB
Open-source multi-model database suitable for modeling entity relationships, ownership structures, corporate hierarchies, and interconnected governance records.

Keycloak
Open-source identity and access-management platform useful for securing self-hosted entity-management systems with SSO, role-based access, groups, and centralized authentication.

Open Policy Agent
Open-source policy engine that can implement machine-readable governance rules, approval policies, access controls, and compliance logic around entity-management workflows.

OpenProject
Open-source project-management platform that can be used to track entity formations, restructurings, mergers, acquisitions, governance projects, compliance remediation, and corporate actions.

Paperless-ngx
Open-source document-management system useful for self-hosted storage, OCR, classification, tagging, and retrieval of incorporation documents, resolutions, certificates, and governance records.

Mayan EDMS
Open-source electronic document-management system suitable for organizing corporate records, legal documents, filings, certificates, board documents, and entity documentation.

Docspell
Open-source document-management system with OCR, tagging, metadata, and search capabilities that can serve as a document layer for entity-management systems.

Nextcloud
Open-source collaboration and file-management platform that can provide a self-hosted document repository for entity records, governance documents, and corporate compliance files.

Directus
Open-source data platform that can provide an API-first interface and administrative UI for structured entity records, ownership data, corporate documents, and governance information.

Strapi
Open-source headless CMS that can be used as a customizable data-management layer for corporate entities, people, documents, jurisdictions, and governance records.

Appsmith
Open-source internal application platform for rapidly building custom entity-management dashboards, compliance workflows, administrative interfaces, and reporting applications.

ToolJet
Open-source low-code platform for creating internal entity-management applications connected to databases and APIs.

Budibase
Open-source internal-tool platform suitable for building custom legal-entity registries, compliance trackers, approval workflows, and governance dashboards.

NocoDB
Open-source Airtable-like database interface that can transform relational entity-management data into collaborative tables, forms, and workflows.

Baserow
Open-source database and no-code platform suitable for building lightweight entity registries, compliance calendars, governance records, and corporate-data workflows.

Supabase
Open-source backend platform built around PostgreSQL, providing authentication, APIs, storage, and database capabilities for custom entity-management applications.

Hasura GraphQL Engine
Open-source GraphQL data platform useful for exposing structured entity, ownership, governance, compliance, and document metadata through APIs.

Apache Airflow
Open-source workflow orchestration platform useful for automating entity-data synchronization, registry imports, compliance checks, reporting, and scheduled governance workflows.

Apache NiFi
Open-source dataflow platform for importing, transforming, reconciling, and synchronizing corporate entity data from multiple registries and enterprise systems.

OpenSearch
Open-source search and analytics engine useful for searching large collections of entity records, governance documents, filings, and compliance information.

OpenSanctions
Open-source data and tooling for sanctions, politically exposed persons, and related entity data that can complement entity due-diligence and compliance workflows.

OpenOwnership
Open-source projects and standards for representing beneficial ownership information, useful for ownership transparency and corporate-structure applications.

Beneficial Ownership Data Standard
Open standard for representing beneficial ownership data, providing a structured foundation for modeling ownership relationships and ultimate beneficial owners.

GLEIF API / LEI Data
Open-source tooling and public data resources from the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation useful for standardized legal-entity identification and entity-data enrichment.

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Entity-management software capabilities, pricing, licensing, jurisdictional coverage, and availability can change over time.

Some projects listed as open-source are complete applications, while others are standards, databases, frameworks, data sources, or infrastructure components that can be combined to build an entity-management platform.

Corporate and regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction. Organizations should independently verify filing, beneficial-ownership, governance, tax, and statutory requirements with qualified professionals and applicable authorities.

Open-source software does not automatically provide regulatory compliance, legal validity, or official corporate-registry access.
